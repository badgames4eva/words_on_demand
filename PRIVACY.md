# Privacy Policy — Words on Demand

**Effective date:** July 27, 2026
**Last updated:** July 27, 2026

Words on Demand ("the app", "we", "us") is a daily word puzzle for televisions and
streaming devices, published by **badgames4eva**. This policy explains what the app
does and does not do with information. It is deliberately short, because the app collects
very little.

Questions: **badgameseva@gmail.com**

---

## The short version

- We do **not** ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, phone number, or any
  account information. The app has no accounts and no login.
- We do **not** operate any server that receives your data. Your game progress stays on
  your device.
- We show **advertisements**, and our advertising provider (Google) receives limited
  technical information in order to deliver those ads. This is the only way information
  leaves your device.
- We serve **non-personalized ads only**. We do not build advertising profiles about you
  and we do not use your advertising ID for interest-based targeting.
- The app is intended for a **general audience**. It is not directed to children under 13.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves your game state locally on your device (in browser `localStorage`, under the
key `wordsondemand.v1`) so you can leave a puzzle and come back to it. This includes:

- Your win/loss statistics and daily streak (games played, games won, current streak)
- The puzzle you are working on and the guesses you have made
- Whether you used a hint, and how long you have spent on the current puzzle

**This information never leaves your device.** We cannot see it. It is not transmitted to
us or to anyone else. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes it
permanently. There is no backup and no way for us to restore it.

## Advertising

The app is free and supported by video advertisements shown between rounds, and by an
optional "watch an ad to reveal a letter" hint that you choose to start. Advertisements
are delivered using **Google Ad Manager** and the Google Interactive Media Ads (IMA) SDK.

To deliver and measure an ad, and to prevent ad fraud, Google may receive:

- Your device's IP address (from which an **approximate**, city-or-region-level location
  can be inferred — never a precise GPS location; the app does not request or have access
  to location permissions)
- Device and technical information such as device type, operating system, screen size, and
  language
- Your device's **advertising identifier**, and information about ad events (that an ad
  was requested, shown, or completed)

**We serve non-personalized ads.** Ads are selected based on general context rather than
an interest profile built from your behavior. We do not use your advertising identifier to
track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your information for
cross-context behavioral advertising.

Google's handling of this information is governed by its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy — https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services —
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can limit ad tracking at the device level. On Fire TV: **Settings → Preferences →
Advertising ID**. On Android TV / Google TV: **Settings → Privacy → Ads**. These controls
are provided by your device, not by us, and using them does not stop ads from appearing —
it limits how your advertising identifier may be used.

## What we do not do

- We do **not** collect personal or contact information.
- We do **not** use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
- We do **not** use cookies for tracking.
- We do **not** access your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, files, or precise
  location. The app requests no such permissions.
- We do **not** sell your personal information.
- We do **not** knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Children's privacy

The app is a general-audience word game and is **not directed to children under 13**. We
do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has
provided personal information through the app, contact us at **badgameseva@gmail.com** and we
will address it — though note that because the app has no server and no accounts, we hold
no such information to begin with.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in
California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your
personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing.

Because the app has no accounts and no backend, **we hold no personal information about
you to access, correct, or delete.** The game data described above is on your device and
fully under your control — clear the app's data to erase it. For information held by
Google as our advertising provider, see the Google links above; Google provides its own
controls and request mechanisms.

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, so
there is no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" action for us to offer.

To ask a privacy question, email **badgameseva@gmail.com**.

## Data security and retention

Because game data never leaves your device, its security depends on your device's own
protections. We retain no user data on any server, so there is nothing for us to retain or
delete. Ad-related information is retained by Google under Google's own retention policies.

## International users

The app has no server, so we do not transfer your information internationally. Google, as
our advertising provider, may process ad-request information in countries other than your
own under its own policies and safeguards.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the
revised version at the URL where you found it. Material changes — for example, if we ever
add analytics or enable personalized advertising — will be reflected here before those
changes take effect.
