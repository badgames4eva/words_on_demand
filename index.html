How to Play

  • Guess the hidden 5-letter word in 6 tries.
  • Use the D-pad to move, OK/Enter to select a letter.
  • Green = right letter, right spot.
  • Yellow = right letter, wrong spot.
  • Gray = not in the word.
  • Green letters carry down to your next guess automatically — no need to retype them.
  • Hold Erase / Rewind to clear a whole row, carried-down greens included.
